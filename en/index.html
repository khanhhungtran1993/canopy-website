About Canopy Vietnam

Science is the root.
Nature is the destination.

Canopy Vietnam (Tán Rừng Việt Nam) is a forestry seedling producer based in Binh Dinh, Vietnam, combining plant tissue-culture technology with traditional cutting propagation to deliver genetically consistent, fast-growing, well-adapted planting stock.

1

Modern tissue-culture technology

Sterile in-vitro propagation with automated contamination screening for disease-free stock from the start.

2

Selective cutting propagation

Sourced from high-performing mother trees to maintain stable genetic traits across generations.

3

Long-term direction

Beyond forestry seedlings, Canopy Vietnam is expanding into higher-value crops and end-to-end plantation support services.

Products

Forestry planting stock

Acacia Hybrid Seedlings

Selected clonal lines (BV10, BV16, BV32...), propagated by cuttings or tissue culture. Fast-growing, suited for pulpwood and woodchip plantations.
